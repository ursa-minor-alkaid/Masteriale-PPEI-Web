02Supervision

Scientific design, guided from Évry Paris-Saclay

Prof. Sylvain Fisson — Professor at Université d'Évry Paris-Saclay and leader of the Sino-French Bioscience Program. His research focuses on gene therapy for ocular diseases and clinical translation, particularly on immune responses to gene therapy and how to regulate them in the immune‑privileged ocular microenvironment to avoid treatment failure, covering the full innovation path from basic mechanisms to clinical application. He was an invited lecturer at the International Summer School of Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST) on 'Gene Therapy for Ocular Diseases and Its Clinical Application'. In this project, he supervised the overall scientific design.

03Acknowledgements

Expertise from the dengue vaccine frontline

Dr Vianney Tricou — Expert in arboviral diagnostics and epidemiology at Takeda Pharmaceuticals International AG (Zurich, Switzerland). He has been deeply involved in international multicentre clinical trials of the tetravalent dengue live‑attenuated vaccine TAK‑003 (branded QDENGA), serving as a core co‑author from Phase II dose‑finding to Phase III pivotal efficacy studies, with results published in top medical journals such as *The Lancet Infectious Diseases*. His profound expertise in dengue viraemia kinetics directly guided this project to focus on a single well‑characterised target for optimised detection strategy.
