01Sino-French Master Program (BTCG)

The Sino-French Master's Program in Tissue, Cell, and Gene Biotherapies (BTCG) is a joint international master's program established by Université Paris-Saclay and Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), delivered entirely in English. The program focuses on the field of biotherapy, covering four major areas: gene therapy, cell therapy, immunotherapy, and innovative pharmacotherapy. It aims to provide students with rigorous training in fundamental research while opening diverse career pathways in the biomedical and pharmaceutical industries. Structured across two semesters, the first emphasizes theoretical and laboratory coursework, while the second involves a six-month internship in an academic laboratory or an international biotech or pharmaceutical company. The program is open to students with backgrounds in science, medicine, pharmacy, or engineering, and graduates may pursue careers in research institutions, biotechnology companies, or continue to doctoral studies.

02Université Paris-Saclay

Université Paris-Saclay is one of France's leading comprehensive research universities. Its predecessor, Université Paris-Sud (Paris XI), was officially renamed in January 2020 as part of a major restructuring that brought together several elite institutions and research centers in the Greater Paris region. The university is internationally renowned for its strengths in physics, mathematics, chemistry, and life sciences. In the 2021 Academic Ranking of World Universities (ARWU), it ranked 13th globally overall, 1st in the world in mathematics, and 9th in physics — both top positions in France. The university operates 83 research laboratories, publishes over 6,300 scientific works annually, and hosts 5,000 international students from 136 countries. It is a flagship institution of France's national higher education reform initiative, the Initiative d'Excellence (IDEX).

03Huazhong University of Science and Technology

Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST) is a key comprehensive research university directly under the Ministry of Education of China, located in Wuhan, Hubei Province. It was established in 2000 through the merger of Huazhong University of Science and Technology (formerly Huazhong Institute of Technology), Tongji Medical College, and Wuhan Urban Construction Institute. HUST is among the first cohort of universities in China's "Double First-Class," "985 Project," and "211 Project" national initiatives. The university covers 11 major academic disciplines, offers 117 undergraduate programs, and holds 46 first-level doctoral degree authorizations. It consistently ranks in the top ten among Chinese universities, with internationally competitive programs in mechanical engineering, optical engineering, and biomedical engineering. Its sprawling 7,000-mu campus, with 72% green coverage, has earned it the nickname "Forest University." The university currently enrolls approximately 60,000 students.
