What A-BITE is not

01A replacement for RT-qPCR 02A test you can use blind 03A proven method 04A treatment 05A complete map 06Research-use-only prototype

01A replacement for RT-qPCR

The device targets the first 12–24 hours after a known bite. Later, the virus is in the blood, and a blood test is the right tool.

02A test you can use blind

You need to know you were bitten, and where. That is why the device goes where alerts are running.

03A proven method

Viral RNA has never been measured in human skin fluid. That is what this project sets out to establish — and a negative result would still be a result.

04A treatment

There is no antiviral for chikungunya. The benefit is telling it apart from dengue, avoiding a long diagnostic detour, and reaching surveillance earlier.

05A complete map

A network only sees where it is installed. Areas without a device stay invisible.

06Research-use-only prototype

No diagnostic claim is made without IVDR conformity.
